# RateHelper v5.0.3

*(Oct 5–6, 2026)*

- **Three-driver mode:** When three people share the car, pick **Three Drivers (Shared)** in Earnings / settings. Rent and weekly goals follow that schedule; you can enter combined car trip count.
- **Fuel split:** When adding a receipt, tap **Split** — divide the pump total among 2–6 people, see **your share**, and save that amount on the receipt.
- **Cancel on overlay:** Optional setting shows a **cancel** button on the pill next to accept/reject.
